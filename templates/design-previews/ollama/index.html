Your data stays yours

Prompts and responses for local models never leave your computer. For cloud models, Ollama does not log, retain or train on your data — and you can turn cloud off entirely in settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ollama free?

Yes. Running models on your own hardware is free and always will be. Paid plans only cover cloud usage.

What hardware do I need?

Any modern Mac, Windows or Linux machine. 8 GB of RAM runs 3B–7B models; larger models benefit from a GPU with 16 GB or more.

Can I use Ollama with my existing tools?

Ollama exposes an OpenAI-compatible API at localhost:11434, so most SDKs and editors work out of the box.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Cancel any time from your account settings, or email hello@ollama.com.
